Section A Very Short Answer Questions (VSAQs)
10 Questions × 2 Marks = 20 Marks No Choice • All Compulsory

Questions 1 to 10. These cover definitions, laws, units, and direct 1-step calculations. Rank decider section: 10 out of 10 correct is mandatory for a 60/60 score!

Section B Short Answer Questions (SAQs)
Answer 6 of 8 Questions × 4 Marks = 24 Marks Choice: 6 out of 8

Questions 11 to 18. Covers 4-mark derivations, short explanations with diagrams, and numerical problems. Students have 2 full choice questions to skip if unfamiliar.

Section C Long Answer Questions (LAQs)
Answer 2 of 3 Questions × 8 Marks = 16 Marks Choice: 2 out of 3

Questions 19 to 21. Strictly from designated high-weightage chapters! Physics: Waves, Current Electricity, Nuclei. Chemistry: p-Block, Electrochemistry/Kinetics, Organic Chemistry.

💡 60/60 Master Strategy decoded from State Toppers:
  • Neat Diagrams: Draw circuit diagrams (Potentiometer, Wheatstone bridge) and apparatus (Nuclear reactor, Organ pipes) with sharp pencil and labels.
  • Chemical Equations: Write fully balanced equations with conditions (catalyst, temp, pressure) in Haber, Ostwald, and Contact processes.
  • Units Matter: Never leave numerical answers without proper SI units ($\text{m/s}, \text{V}, \text{Hz}, \text{S cm}^2\text{mol}^{-1}$).

1. Waves: Stretched Strings / Sonometer / Organ Pipes Repeated 6 Times (2024, 2023, 2022, 2020)

2. Current Electricity: Potentiometer / Wheatstone Bridge Repeated 6 Times (2024, 2023, 2022, 2019)

Principle of potentiometer + comparison of EMF of 2 cells ($E_1/E_2 = l_1/l_2$) or internal resistance ($r = R(l_1-l_2)/l_2$); Kirchhoff's laws & Wheatstone bridge balance ($P/Q = R/S$).

3. Nuclei: Nuclear Reactor / Radioactive Decay Law Repeated 6 Times (2024, 2023, 2022, 2020)

Principle, neat labeled diagram and components of a Nuclear Reactor (Fuel, Moderator, Control rods, Coolant, Shield) or $N = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$ derivation and half-life.

4. Chemistry p-Block: Haber / Ostwald / Contact / Ozone Repeated 7 Times (Guaranteed Every Year)

Manufacture of Ammonia ($N_2 + 3H_2 \rightleftharpoons 2NH_3$) with Le Chatelier's conditions (200 atm, 700 K, Fe) or Ozone laboratory preparation and reactions with $PbS, KI, Ag$.

5. Chemistry Organic: High-Yield Named Reactions Repeated 6 Times (Section C Q21)

Reimer-Tiemann, Kolbe's, Williamson's synthesis, Cannizzaro, Aldol condensation, HVZ, Carbylamine test, Hoffmann bromamide degradation.

6. Electrochemistry & Chemical Kinetics (4M + 4M Combo) Repeated 5 Times
